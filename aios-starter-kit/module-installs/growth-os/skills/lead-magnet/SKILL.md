# Lead Magnet

Design — and draft — a lead magnet that pulls the user's exact ICP, then produce the real asset, not a description of one.

## Gather

1. **ICP + their pain** — who the user wants on their list and the specific problem that ICP loses sleep over (from `context/*.md`).
2. **What the user sells** — so the magnet is a natural on-ramp to it (solves a slice of the same problem).
3. **Format preference** — checklist, template, mini-guide, swipe file, calculator, teardown. Pick the lightest format that delivers a fast win if unspecified.

## Output

1. **The pitch** (2-3 lines): the magnet's title + the one specific outcome it gives ("Get X in 10 minutes without Y").
2. **Why it converts**: one line on the ICP pain it resolves and how it leads to the paid offer.
3. **The actual asset, drafted in full** — the checklist items, the template, the guide sections. Real and usable, not an outline.
4. **The opt-in copy**: headline + 2-line subhead + button text for the capture page.

## Rules

- **Fast, specific win.** A magnet that solves one sharp problem in 10 minutes beats a 40-page "ultimate guide" nobody finishes.
- **On-ramp, not the whole journey.** It should make the paid offer the obvious next step, not replace it.
- **Title promises a concrete outcome**, not a topic. "The 7-point cold-email teardown" > "Cold email tips".
- Match the user's voice and real expertise; pull from their material.

## After drafting

Offer to save the asset to `outputs/`, draft the email that delivers it, or design the 3-email nurture sequence that follows.
